Data protection

Under the Data Protection Act, 2021, your personal information is used solely for union purposes. You may request a copy of your data or ask us to correct it by contacting the Membership Office.

Withdraw membership

You may withdraw at any time under the Industrial and Labour Relations Act, Cap 269. Contact the Membership Office to begin; a withdrawal notice will be prepared and District HR / PMEC informed to stop the deduction.
