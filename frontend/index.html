Switching to PUSEWU is your right.

How to join

Apply and sign online. Your salary does the rest.

No cash. No mobile money. Your subscription is a clean payroll deduction handled through PMEC.

Apply & sign online

Complete the digital consent form and sign it with your finger, verified by an SMS code. Legally valid under the ECT Act, 2021.

PUSEWU approves

An officer verifies your eligibility — typically within 24–48 hours — and issues your membership number and digital card.

PMEC deducts K150

We submit your details to PMEC under deduction code F396. K150 comes off your payroll monthly — nothing for you to do.

You track everything

Your dashboard shows your deduction history, your growing K20-a-month retirement savings, and any case you lodge.

Already in another union? Tell us during sign-up. We generate your withdrawal notice under Cap 269 (s.5(f) & s.22(2)), notify District HR and PMEC, and switch your deduction to PUSEWU — no double deductions.
